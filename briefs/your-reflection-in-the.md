# Your Reflection in the Mirror

- **Reel folder:** `reels/your-reflection-in-the/`
- **Length:** ~45 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps (tight: each beat = its line + a breath, no dead air between scenes)
- **Look:** soft 3D low-poly toy diorama
- **Voice:** `audio/your-reflection-in-the-vo.wav` — narrator ElevenLabs v3 "Chris"; the reflection = Chris pitched down a little with a glassy echo (unless you record it)
- **Reuses from `bucket-shared/`:** `toy-kit`, `rig-toy-person` (you + reflection), `world-bathroom` (mirror anchor), `props`, `synth-toy`, `voice/`. New builders: `world-ocean` (sea + toy boat), props (hammer, sledgehammer, chainsaw, bomb, hand mirror), `mirrored` flag on the person rig.

## Hook (0–3 s)

Close on the bathroom mirror. You wave. Your reflection doesn't. It folds its arms, raises one eyebrow and slowly smirks straight into the lens. Text slams in. The viewer stays because something is clearly very wrong with the mirror.

## End card

41–45 s. Toy boat bobbing at sunset, you in sunglasses. Text pops letter by letter: **REFLECT ON THAT.** In the final 10 frames the calm water shows your reflection again — copying you perfectly now — and it gives a tiny defeated thumbs-up.

## Look notes

- Same toy kit as the head-lice reel: flat-shaded pastel primitives, soft key light, soft shadows, light tilt-shift; same bathroom set.
- The reflection: same toy person, mirrored, with a faint cool glass tint and a subtle shimmer; a smug face (one brow up, side smirk).
- Ocean: faceted low-poly waves (summed sines, flat-shaded), turquoise → deep blue, little white foam caps; toy boat with a striped sail.
- Impacts read through squash-and-stretch, sparks, bounce-backs and pops; the mirror always ends spotless with a "ting" glint.

## Safe zones

Keep text and key action inside x 60–940, y 250–1500 (platform UI covers the top, bottom and right edge).

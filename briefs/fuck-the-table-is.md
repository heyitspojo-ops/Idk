# Fuck, the Table Is Uneven

- **Reel folder:** `reels/fuck-the-table-is/`
- **Length:** ~45 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps (tight: each beat = its line + a breath)
- **Look:** soft 3D low-poly toy diorama
- **Voice:** `audio/fuck-the-table-is-vo.wav` — narrator ElevenLabs v3 "Chris" (the cat never speaks)
- **Reuses from `bucket-shared/`:** `toy-kit`, `rig-toy-person`, `world-house`, `world-street`, `props`, `synth-toy`, `vo-warp`, `voice/`. New builders: `rig-cat`, `world-planet` (with sun + spiral galaxy), props (wobbly table, giant nails, steel beams, giant bolts, wrench, folded napkin), group shake, synth SFX (clang, rumble).

## Hook (0–3 s)

Close on a coffee mug on a little table: the table rocks — clack, clack — coffee sloshing over the rim with every tip. Pull back to you staring at it with murder in your eyes. Title slams in.

## End card

45–47 s. Empty living room, a square dust mark where the table was, you standing in it with a big relieved grin; through the doorway the cat drags the table down the street. Text pops letter by letter: **TECHNICALLY FIXED.** Last 10 frames: the cat looks back at the lens and slow-blinks.

## Look notes

- Same toy kit as the other reels: flat-shaded pastel primitives, soft key light, soft shadows, light tilt-shift; reuse the living room and street.
- Table: small round wooden table, one leg visibly shorter; mug with a steaming coffee surface that sloshes.
- Cat: chunky grey tabby, big round head, triangle ears, flat half-lidded "unimpressed" eyes; "pissed" = ears flat, narrowed eyes, tail puffed.
- Escalation reads through scale: nails → steel beams → giant bolts; wobble grows from a rock to a quake to a planet sway.
- Planet: faceted blue-green Earth with white cloud puffs, a tiny house + bolts on top, starfield, a little moon.

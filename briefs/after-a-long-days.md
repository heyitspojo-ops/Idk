- **Reel folder:** `reels/after-a-long-days/`
- **Length:** ~45–55 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps (tight: each beat = its line + a breath)
- **Look:** soft 3D low-poly toy diorama · bouncy, snappy motion · dynamic punchy camera (`motion-fx`)

## Hook (0–3 s)

Your bedroom at night, lamp off, you snoring under the blanket. The blanket on the other side slowly lifts… a grinning stranger slides in next to you. Your eyes snap open. Title slams in with a camera punch.

48–50 s. Sunrise in the bedroom: you lying rigid, eyes huge, a patched hole above you and bolts on the window; the creep asleep on your chest, smiling. Text pops letter by letter: **NOT SWEET.** Last 10 frames: his eyes snap open and look straight at the lens.

## Look notes

- Same toy kit; the house becomes a cosy bedroom (bed, lamp, window with curtains, a door with locks, a ceiling with a light).
- Night = deep blue key light + a warm bedside lamp; Night 2/3/4 cards; the final morning = warm sunrise.
- The creep: lanky, striped shirt, slicked hair, permanent wide grin, half-lidded eyes; moves with bouncy, slinky hops.
- All contact stays cartoon-cuddly and non-sexual (spooning, hugging, a hand patting your hair); the comedy is his creepiness and your fury.
- Kick-outs: fast cartoon boots with a camera punch; he flies out in an arc and bounces down the street.

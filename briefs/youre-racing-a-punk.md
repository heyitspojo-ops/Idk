# You're Racing a Punk Ass Honda Civic

- **Reel folder:** `reels/youre-racing-a-punk/`
- **Length:** ~60 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps (tight: each beat = its line + a breath)
- **Look:** soft 3D low-poly toy diorama
- **Voice:** `audio/youre-racing-a-punk-vo.wav` — narrator ElevenLabs v3 "Chris" (the Civic driver never speaks)
- **Reuses from `bucket-shared/`:** `toy-kit`, `rig-toy-person` (you + Civic driver), `world-street`, `props`, `synth-toy`, `vo-warp`, `voice/`. New builders: `car-toy` (beater sedan / hatch, detachable doors, hood scoop, engine size, chassis twist), `world-drag-strip`, `world-usa-map`, `world-swamp`, props (NOS bottle, engine parts, trophy), synth SFX (engine drone, NOS hiss).
- **Note:** the Civic is a generic toy hatchback (no logos); "Vin Diesel" is a VO line only, no likeness.

## Hook (0–3 s)

Low on the drag strip: two toy cars side by side at the start tree — your rusty beige sedan coughing smoke, and a lime-green hatch with a giant spoiler. The hatch driver looks over and smirks. Lights go red… yellow… Title slams in.

- Your car: boxy beige 90s sedan, rust patches, one hubcap missing, exhaust smoke; later doorless, then with a chrome supercharger + red V8 towering out of the hood.
- The Civic: lime-green compact hatch, big wing spoiler, tinted windows, neon underglow.
- Drag strip: grey asphalt, white lane lines, Christmas-tree start lights, grandstand of tiny toy fans.
- US map: a pastel low-poly continental US on a table, states as raised tiles, dotted flight arc.
- Swamp: green-brown water, mangrove roots, lily pads, a chunky toy gator, a pink flamingo, Spanish-moss trees.
